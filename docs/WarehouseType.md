# WarehouseType

The kind of data platform a warehouse represents.  Every connection on a warehouse has to fit its type. A `DATA_LAKE` warehouse holds the connections of a lake made of several systems, such as a metastore and a query engine. A `CUSTOM_INTEGRATION` warehouse holds connections you implement yourself.
