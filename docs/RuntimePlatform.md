# RuntimePlatform

Where a deployment's collection agent or data store runs.  A proxied platform — `AWS_PROXIED` or `GCP_PROXIED` — is one Monte Carlo reaches over HTTPS through a proxy or gateway you run, instead of calling the cloud provider directly. `GENERIC` and `SNOWFLAKE` are not called at all: they connect out to Monte Carlo.
